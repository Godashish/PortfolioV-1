About

Connecting financial policy with applied research for South Asia's development needs.

I work at the intersection of public finance institutions and evidence-based policy. My professional experience includes designing credit guarantee instruments for smallholder farmers and MSMEs, leading financial recovery audits across multi-country supply chains, and producing applied research on climate finance, monetary policy spillovers, and stock market dynamics in developing economies.

Fluent in Nepali and English, I bring regional context to institutional work and international collaboration.
